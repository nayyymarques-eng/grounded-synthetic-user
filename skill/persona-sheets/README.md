# Persona sheets

One page per role. Each lists the role's traits, such as their day, what they fear, how they judge a screen and the words they use. Each trait carries:

- **Its source**: the interview, quote or note it came from. One row per interview, never a name.
- **Its strength**: how many interviews say it.

A trait heard second-hand, taken from desk research or written as a hypothesis is always weak. A finding that rests only on weak traits is marked low evidence, and it becomes a question for the next interview instead of a fix.

**Set for your business:** how many interviews make a trait strong. The example uses 2.

Three more rules keep a sheet honest:

- Where two interviews disagree, keep both. They may show two kinds of user, each with its own sheet.
- A pain that comes from one company's history is marked as that company's, not as a trait of the role.
- What nobody said is written as a hypothesis, so the agent cannot speak for users on it.

A role with no interviews can still run. Its sheet is marked low evidence, and so is every finding it reports.

The two sheets here are examples with made-up data. Replace them with sheets built from your own interviews.
